# Changelog

All notable changes to the Crypto Advertising and Promotion Rules by Country dataset.

## [1.0] - 2026-11-24 (2026-Q4)

### Added
- First release: 58 rules across 37 jurisdictions (36 countries and territories plus the EU).
- All 32 Tier 1 jurisdictions, including EU-level MiCA rows and national MiCA frameworks for Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands and Ireland.
- First Tier 2 jurisdictions: China, Russia, Taiwan, Pakistan, Qatar.
- Sub-national and free-zone rows for New York (NYDFS), Dubai (VARA) and Abu Dhabi Global Market (FSRA).
- 39 rows verified from primary sources; 19 rows marked unverified with reasons in `notes`.
- Codebook, README, citation file and DataCite-style metadata.

### Known gaps
- Remaining EU/EEA member states and other Tier 2 and Tier 3 jurisdictions.
- US CFTC rules not yet researched.
